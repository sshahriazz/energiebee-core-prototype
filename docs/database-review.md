# EnergieBee core: database review

Read-only review of the data structure and database design of `energiebee-core-prototype`, 26 October 2026.
Scope: `src/db/schema.sql`, `src/db/db.js`, deltas 002–010, `src/db/seed/*.js`, `src/db/registry.js`, `docs/database.md`, and the SQL in `src/core`, `src/jobs` and `src/server.js`.

**How it was checked.** The live `data/core.db` was never opened for writing. A consistent copy was taken with `VACUUM INTO` from a read-only connection at 05:09 UTC (a second copy at 05:17 UTC for the raw-file check). All queries, plans and timings ran on copies in a scratch folder. A fresh database was built by importing `src/db/db.js` with `DB_FILE` pointing at an empty file. Every SQL statement run by a full rebuild, the forecast, trust, compare and 23 GET endpoints was captured by wrapping `StatementSync`, then run through `EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN`. External network calls were blocked (`fetch` stubbed); the GET endpoints were served on 127.0.0.1 from the copy. Runtime: Node 24.22.1, SQLite 3.51 (the project asks for Node >= 22.13).

**In flux, not reviewed in depth.** Another agent is adding the tounify adapter: `src/db/deltas/011-tounify.sql`, `src/adapters/tounify.js` and related files were changing during this review (for example, `docs/database.md` and `src/jobs/steps.js` changed while it ran). Delta 011 is mentioned only where it touches a general finding (H5). Line numbers are as read on 26 October.

---

## (a) Executive summary

1. **Overall: 5 / 10.** The fact layer is well designed for one home. The operations, privacy and scale layers are not ready for a second home.
2. **Integrity 5/10:** foreign keys are on and the live data is clean (`integrity_check` ok, `foreign_key_check` empty, no overlaps), but the overlap triggers fire only on INSERT, consistency triggers ignore NULLs, and the schema accepts wrong units and types.
3. **Time and units 6/10:** UTC is used throughout and clock changes are handled correctly (25 Oct has 50 slots), but seconds and milliseconds share the `_at` suffix and nothing checks which one a value is in.
4. **Versioning 6/10:** readings, intervals, rates, agreements and tax rules are correctly bitemporal, but forecasts have no recorded time, and site, tariff, metric and market settings are overwritten in place, so "as known at" rebuilds use today's settings.
5. **Indexing and performance 5/10:** fast today (the whole traced workload spends 275 ms in SQL), but several hot reads scan a series' whole history, and "as known at" reads cannot use any index.
6. **Generality of the model 6/10:** market, region, tax rule, register window and calendar are a sound general design, but two vocabularies share a column in places, region series bypass the region table, and CHECKs block 5-minute slots.
7. **Migrations and operations 4/10:** three historic schemas upgrade to the current one exactly, but every open writes 163 rows and changes the schema three times, with no busy timeout. A second process fails at once with "database is locked". Raw payloads, forecast issues and sync logs are never pruned.
8. **Security and privacy 3/10:** no credentials were found in the database or the raw files, but email, phone, address, MPAN/MPRN, the account number and the home's coordinates sit in plain files that are kept for ever and served without login.
9. **Scale readiness 3/10:** estimated 1.2 MB of database and 16–24 MB of raw files per home per day. That is about 0.45 TB and 6–9 TB a year for 1,000 homes, with one writer, no `site_id` on facts and no tenant model.
10. **Findings:** 1 critical, 5 high, 13 medium, 8 low. Fix C1, H1, H2 and H4 now. Most of the rest must be fixed before a second home.

## (c) What is good

- **The facts are truly append-only and bitemporal.** `putReadings` and `putIntervals` supersede rather than overwrite. `inForce()` uses the right predicate (`recorded_at <= T AND (superseded_at IS NULL OR superseded_at > T)`). The live copy has 0 keys with two current versions. 459 superseded readings and 2,133 superseded intervals are kept.
- **Clean live data.** `PRAGMA integrity_check` = ok. `foreign_key_check` is empty. There are 0 overlapping current rates and 0 overlapping current agreements. No fact lacks its series. No series points to a missing site or device. Every `site_day.slots` equals its `site_hh` count.
- **Migrations converge.** Schemas from commits `56778bc`, `85eed5a` and `7cd804f` each upgrade through `db.js` to the same 58 objects as a fresh database. Only column order differs (L5).
- **Clock changes are right.** 25 Oct 2026 has 50 slots, and local midnight has `tod_min` 0 on 24, 25 and 26 Oct. The Sunsynk normaliser places the repeated 01:00–01:59 hour (`sunsynk.js:62-70`).
- **Foreign keys are on** on the only application connection (`PRAGMA foreign_keys` = 1, `db.js:21`). Currency and market triggers, partial unique indexes for "one current row", and CHECKed enums exist for tax kinds, components, provenance origins and register windows.
- **The general model is sound in shape.** `market`, `region` and `region_alias` (with partition-alias triggers), `tax_rule` with sequenced percent, per-kWh and per-period kinds, `register_window` + `calendar_day` for TOU and Tempo, and `provenance` origins on every reference row.
- **No credentials are stored.** `connectProvider` keeps only non-secret references (`secrets_stored: false`). `provider_state` holds no token. A key-name scan of the raw files and the request column found no token, password or API-key fields.
- **Serving tables are disposable and deterministic.** The full rebuild is 140 ms, and a one-day rebuild 6 ms, on the copy.
