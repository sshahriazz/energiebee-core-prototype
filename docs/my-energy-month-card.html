How the card is worked out

Three steps, repeated every 10 minutes.

1

Collect the data

Inverter solar, battery, electricity
Smart meter electricity, gas
Octopus tariffs prices
National Gas gas energy content
VAT rules 0% electricity, 5% gas
2

Price every half-hour

48 times a day, the app works out what that half-hour cost: electricity bought + gas used + a share of the standing charges − export paid.

3

Add them up

The card adds every half-hour from 1 October until now: half-hours.
