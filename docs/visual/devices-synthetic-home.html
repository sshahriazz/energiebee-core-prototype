Is the synthetic home's data right?

What the five demo devices send, how the core turns it into energy, whether each number on the page matches, and what needs fixing, explained one step at a time.

Synthetic IoT home (demo data), home 2 · history 27 Oct 10:05 to 30 Oct 10:05 (UK time, GMT) · every value was regenerated from the formulas and compared with what the core stored and what the page receives

The short answer

The data is right, down to the last reading. A few things on the page are worded or drawn in a way that can mislead.

✓

Every reading matches

All 14 channels of the 5 devices, every 5 minutes for 3 days, are exactly what the generator's formulas give. Nothing is missing and nothing extra was stored.

12,072 / 12,072readings identical
✓

The energy adds up

Every period of every counter is the difference of the two readings around it. The four built-in faults are each handled the right way.

3,436 / 3,436energy periods correct
!

But 5 things can mislead

None of them changes a number. The biggest: the battery's evening discharge never shows as power, and its settings don't match how it behaves.

5display and wording fixes

"No new data, last data 36 minutes ago" on every device is expected: the history deliberately stops 30 minutes before the home was created, so you can press "Simulate next period" to catch up.

How the data is made and shown

Three steps. The data is fake, but steps 2 and 3 are the real ones a real device goes through.

1

Generate

Every 5 minutes, each device's value is worked out from the time of day by a fixed formula with a little fixed "noise". There is no random number, so the same time always gives the same value. Code: src/core/synthetic-iot.js.

2

Ingest

The readings go in through ingestObservations, like a real device's. Channels turn each point into a standard measurement. Counters become energy per period, and bad or unknown points are refused.

3

Show

The Devices page reads it back: a card per device with its latest values, then charts, notes "in words", the channel table and the refused readings. Code: src/core/devices-view.js.

What to fix: 5 problems

None of these makes a stored number wrong. They're about what the page leaves out, or how it words or draws something. Biggest first.

1

The battery's discharge never shows as power

Medium

From 17:00 to 23:00 the battery's power chart reads 0 W, while its charge level falls from 99% to 41%. About 5.8 kWh leaves the battery each evening, and the page never shows it as power.

The page shows, 29 Oct 17:00–23:00
0 W
"Charging power" is the only power channel.
What actually happened
5.84 kWh out
700–1,300 W for 6 hours (99.05% → 40.67% of 10 kWh)

Why: the generator works out the discharge but only sends charge_power, and the channel keeps only positive values (part: 'positive'). The discharge is used to move the charge level and then dropped.

Fix: send a discharge_power point (or one signed battery power, split into two channels) in stepAll, and add its channel in createSyntheticIotHome.
2

The battery's settings don't match how it behaves

Medium

The settings panel says the battery keeps a 4% reserve, charges to 100% and has no charge window. The battery actually charges from 00:30 to 98% and stops discharging at 12%.

The settings panel says
reserve 4% · target 100%
window 00:00–00:00
charge and discharge rate 3,000 W
The battery's data shows
floor 12% · stops at 98%
charges from 00:30
charges at 2,000 W, discharges at 700–1,300 W

Why: the settings come from the simulated control driver's defaults (createSimulatedDriver), and the charge level comes from a separate rule in batteryAt. The two were never matched. Anyone testing a command against these settings will see behaviour they don't explain.

Fix: start the driver with the generator's own values (reserve 12, target 98, window 30–300 minutes, rate 2,000 W), or make batteryAt read the driver's settings.
3

The plug's normal midnight reset is called a "Restart"

Low

With 3 days showing, the page lists 4 restarts. Three of them are just the plug's counter starting a new day, yet each one says "the energy used between the last reading and the restart is unknown". It reads like a fault happening every night.

The page says
4 × "Restart"
28 Oct 00:00, 29 Oct 00:00, 29 Oct 14:05, 30 Oct 00:00
It should say
3 × daily reset
1 × restart
only 29 Oct 14:05 is a real restart

Why: the core marks every drop in a counter as partial, which is the safe choice for the data. The page then words every partial period as a restart.

Fix: for a counter whose contract says it resets daily, call a drop at local midnight a "daily reset" in explanationsHtml, and keep "Restart" for drops at any other time.
4

The unmapped point shows the wrong time and no value

Low

The sensor sent firmware_temp = 41 on 27 Oct at 11:05. "Refused readings" lists it at 30 Oct 10:36, when the home was created, with no value.

The page shows
30 Oct 10:36 · no value
"Unmapped point: reported, not stored"
The device sent
27 Oct 11:05 · 41
a single reading

Why: src/core/channels.js:172 records each unmapped point with the time it was refused (nowSec()) and a null value. That's deliberate, to keep one row per point instead of flooding, but the time looks like the reading's time.

Fix: keep one row per point, but store the first reading's time and value, or label the column "refused at" for this reason.
5

The heat gap is drawn as a full-height block

Low

The 6.144 kWh of the 100-minute gap is drawn as one bar. It's more than 11 times the tallest normal bar, so it's cut off at the top of the chart and looks like 100 minutes of maximum output.

The chart draws
a full-height bar
10:15–11:55; the tallest normal bar is 0.542 kWh
On the same scale it is
0.307 kWh per 5 min
6.144 ÷ 20, close to the usual 0.43

Why: counterEnergyChart sets each bar's height from the period's total kWh. A 5-minute bar and a 100-minute bar are drawn on the same scale, and the scale leaves gap bars out.

Fix: draw a gap bar's height as its average per 5 minutes (value ÷ number of 5-minute steps), hatched. Keep the total in the tooltip as it is now.

Check it yourself

Run these in a terminal in the project folder. They only read: -readonly means nothing is changed. Home 2 is the synthetic home in this database. The dates come out in UTC, which is the same as UK time until the clocks go forward in March.
