What the commit adds

Right after the postcode, the user picks who supplies their electricity, then their tariff. That gives the home its prices; the inverter or meter then measures how much is used.

1

Postcode

Gives the home's electricity region (e.g. M = Yorkshire).

2

Supplier

Only suppliers with tariffs in that region. Octopus users are offered the Octopus login instead.

3

Tariff + questions

A single price or day/night. For day/night: when the cheap night rate runs (we fill in the usual hours).

4

Priced

From the home's first day of data. Every half-hour is rebuilt with the new price.

What's wrong: 7 problems

The calculation itself has no errors. The problems are about the data the source gives us, the countries that aren't usable yet, and a few small bugs.

France and the Netherlands can't use the picker Serious

What happens

All 8 French homes get "not available": tounify's licence is unconfirmed, and there's no tounify login in .env.

So

The FR/NL half of the commit only runs in tests with fake data. Check it against live tounify before relying on it.

Some "current" prices are old Medium

What happens

436 of 6,939 tariffs on offer (6%) have a price that started in 2023–25 and never changed. E.g. British Gas Standard Variable (dual fuel, day/night) has been at 35.29p since Dec 2023.

So

A standard variable price normally changes every quarter, so these are probably stale. The picker shows them as current. Hide them, or flag tariffs whose price is over a year old.

The VAT basis is assumed Medium

What happens

The source note says "the API does not state the VAT basis". Prices are treated as before VAT.

So

With 0% VAT on electricity since 1 Oct it makes no difference now. For any date with VAT, prices could be 5% off. Confirm with Tariffscanner.

Night hours aren't checked Medium

What happens

01:00–03:00 (2 hours) is accepted for Economy 7, which is normally 7 hours. The French "exactly 8 hours" is only help text, not a check.

So

A typo gives the home the wrong night/day split for every day since the start. Check the length per market.

Old night hours stay after switching Small

What happens

Switching from Economy 7 to a single-price tariff leaves the 23:00–07:00 hours active. The summary then shows "cheap rate 23:00–07:00" on a single-price tariff.

So

The price isn't affected; it's misleading text. End the meter's hours when the new tariff is single-price.

A future start date is accepted Small

What happens

Choosing with a start date of 1 Jun 2027 works. On a first choice, every day before then has no price.

So

Fine for "I switch next month", but not for a first tariff. Refuse future dates when nothing comes before.

Octopus smart tariffs aren't in the list Small

What happens

Tariffscanner only has Octopus's standard and fixed tariffs, not Agile, Go or Intelligent.

So

The screen already says "connect your Octopus account instead". Users who skip that can't find their tariff.

Check it yourself

  1. Copy the database: sqlite3 data/core.db ".backup /tmp/x/core.db", then run with DB_FILE=/tmp/x/core.db so the real one is never touched.
  2. List suppliers and tariffs: GET /api/sites/9/suppliers and GET /api/sites/9/suppliers/octopus-energy/tariffs.
  3. Pick one: POST /api/sites/9/tariff with {"supplier":"octopus-energy","tariff":"TS-504-E-day_night-E-23"}.
  4. Compare by hand: SELECT local_date, SUM(grid_import*import_rate_ex) FROM site_hh WHERE site_id=9 GROUP BY 1 plus the standing charge, against site_day.total_cost.
  5. Find old prices: tariffs whose current unit row has valid_from before 2026.
